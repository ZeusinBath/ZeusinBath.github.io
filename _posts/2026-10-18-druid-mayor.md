## Mayor's theme: a Druid perspective

*Bath IFG members were invited to respond from different faith perspectives to the Mayor's theme for 2026 "celebrating culture, community and curiosity". Matt McCabe contributed from the Druid tradition:*


![Image Matt McCabe is Pendragon of British Druids](/assets/images/matt.jpg)

*Matt McCabe is the Pendragon of British Druids*

In Druidry we are asked how we will be of service to our community. For some that means their Druid community, but for others that means the community they live in. I am obviously one of the latter, serving as a local councillor.

I believe that the ability to be endlessly curious about the world and all those who dwell here, is one of our greatest strengths as human beings.

The path of Druidry is about exploring your faith in the natural world. It is an experiential tradition – the world throws experiences at you and you are expected to learn from your responses, and work out what it is you believe in. So, being of service to your community affords you the opportunity to broaden your range of experiences, and to widen your self knowledge. Above all it is a curiosity about ‘other’ that leads the Druid into a fascination and celebration of cultures; whether that is ancient cultures, one’s own culture, or the cultures of neighbours you haven’t yet explored.

The Druid is often excited about the thing they have just learnt, on the understanding that the day you stop learning is the day you die.

So being curious just means you’re alive.

Being curious about someone, about their culture, about their community, might just benefit our own community and our own culture, and it will certainly benefit us on our path to the Great Centre.
